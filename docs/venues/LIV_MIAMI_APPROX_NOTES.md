# LIV Miami (approximate recreation) - notes

**APPROXIMATE RECREATION of LIV Miami (Fontainebleau Miami Beach), built from
public 2009 trade press. It is NOT LIV's real patch, plot or addressing, and is
not endorsed by or affiliated with the venue.**

Nothing here came from the venue, its designers or its console. Positions,
addresses, universe split and several fixture counts are invented so the room
can be used as a test rig. Do not use this sheet to patch, address or programme
anything in the real room.

- Preset: `liv_miami_approx` in `backend/app/services/demo/venue_presets.py`
- Patch sheet: `docs/venues/liv_miami_approx_patch.csv` and the same rows to
  read in `docs/venues/liv_miami_approx_patch.md`, both generated by
  `scripts/export_demo_patch_sheet.py` (edit the preset, then re-run it)
- It is the default demo rig: `DEMO_VENUE_PRESET` in `backend/app/main.py` and
  `DEFAULT_DEMO_PRESET` in `backend/app/api/endpoints/demo.py`

project write-up, as summarised in the brief this was built from. The articles
were not re-read while building this, so treat each line as "reported", not
checked.

## What is assumed

The press gives fixture types for these but no counts or positions:

- **Opti Tri Pars (10):** three up each side wall and two at the foot of the
  staircase, all on the floor, and two over the booth as its wash.
- **DLED 60 strips (8):** four across the DJ booth front, two on each side
  wall at skybox height.
- **Atomic 3000 strobes (8):** one off each end of each of the four spot
  trusses.
- **Spoke count (16) and rim segment count (12):** the press gives node and
  flood totals, not how they are divided.
- **Every height and every X/Z position.** Only the 10 ft wash trim and the
  88 ft dome are from the sources. The 6.5 m spot trim, the dome rising from
  7.0 m at the rim to 8.6 m near the centre, and the 4.0 m mezzanine soffit are
  guesses at a plausible room.
- **All DMX addressing and the universe split.**
- **The LED walls' size, layout and make, and the booth wash.** See "LED walls
  and booth".
- **Every room shell dimension except the dome's 88 ft.** See "Room shell".

## What was changed to fit this system

- **The dome is folded down.** 885 dome fixtures patched one for one would
  need about 21 universes at this system's 12-channel footprint; rig universes
  stop at 15, and the 3D view would be drawing 885 lights. So the dome is 44
  fixtures. Each one stands for the group named in the patch sheet's "Stands
  For" column. The matrix can show a radial chase or a two-ring pulse, not
  video.
- **The spots hang on four straight trusses, not a ring.** The engine groups
  moving heads by depth and spreads each movement across a row; the 3D view
  draws one truss per row. A ring of 22 would be eleven rows of two heads with
  nothing to fan across. The four chords (5, 6, 6 and 5 heads) fit inside the
  dome circle. The washes are two rows of four for the same reason.
- **The dome cells and rim segments are patched as generic bars and pars.**
  That is the closest type the preset vocabulary has. In the 3D view they are
  drawn as battens and par cans hung from the ceiling on pipes, which is not
  what a cove node or a flood looks like.
- **The room is a box in the 3D view.** The view builds a rectangular room
  from the stage size; it has no round walls or dome. The fixtures sit on the
  dome's circle but the walls around them are square.
- **The booth is upstage (-Z) and the mezzanine is at the rear (+Z).** That is
  this system's convention, not a claim about which way the real room faces.

## Modernised

The sources describe the 2009 installation. The room has been refurbished
since and the current rig was not researched, so this is not a claim about what
hangs there today. Where a 2009 fixture does not fit how this system drives a
rig, it is patched as the modern LED fixture of the same job:

| 2009 fixture | Patched as | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design Spot 575E / 300E (discharge lamp, mechanical colour) | Generic LED moving spot with RGBW mixing, 16 / 12 channels | The demo profiles mix colour in RGBW; a lamp fixture's wheel and mechanical dimmer are not modelled |
| Impression RGB | Generic RGBW moving wash with zoom, 14 channels | Adds the white emitter current washes have |
| Atomic 3000 DMX (xenon) | Generic 4-channel LED strobe | No xenon recharge or duty-cycle limit is modelled |
| iColor Cove MX, ColorBlast 12 (RGB) | Generic 12-channel RGBW+A+UV cells on DMX | The originals are fed through Color Kinetics power/data supplies, which this system does not model |
| Opti Tri Par, DLED 60 strip (RGB) | Generic 12-channel RGBW+A+UV par / bar | Same footprint as every other static fixture in the demo rigs |

If this room is ever patched in the database instead of run as a demo preset,
the bundled fixture library (`data/fixtures/bundled/`) has a current profile
of the same type for each job. These are stand-ins picked by fixture type, not
what the venue uses:

| Job | Bundled profile | Library type |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dance-floor and mezzanine spots | Elation Platinum FWD | spot |
| Dome centre washes | Elation Fuze Wash 500 | wash |
| Pars, dome rim floods | Elation SixPar 200 | led_par |
| Strips, dome spoke cells | Chauvet DJ COLORband PiX-M USB | led_bar |
| Strobes | Martin RUSH Strobe CW | strobe |

Their channel counts differ from the demo footprints, so the addresses in the
patch sheet would have to be redone.

Every fixture in the preset therefore uses this system's generic demo channel map for its
type. None uses the source fixture's real DMX personality, so the addresses in
the patch sheet would not drive the real fixtures even if the positions were
right.

## LED walls and booth

The press says the DJ booth has LED walls. It gives no size, make, resolution
or signal path, and a video wall is fed by a media server, not patched as
lighting. What is here is a stand-in the engine can colour:

- **27 cells on universe 3**: a centre wall 5 cells wide by 3 high and a wing
  each side 2 wide by 3 high, each cell about a metre wide and 0.8 m high,
  from 3.2 to 5.6 m up the upstage wall. Each cell is a generic 12-channel
  bar. The count, the layout and the three-wall arrangement are assumed.
- **2 booth wash pars** on the same universe, at 6.0 m above the wall either
  side of centre. The press lists no booth wash; they are patched as Opti Tri
  Pars because that is the par the press names.
- The 4 booth front strips from the first pass stay on universe 1, so the
  `dj_booth` group spans universes 1 and 3.

Two things about this system set the wall's shape, not the room:

- It starts at 3.2 m. The 3D view's DJ camera stands behind the upstage wall
  at 2.6 m and looks out through it, and the view's speaker stacks and DJ
  reach about 2.1 m. Cells lower down would sit in front of that camera.
- The centre wall has an odd number of columns. The engine splits a rig into
  left, centre and right half a metre either side of its middle, and a
  column at exactly 0.5 m could land on either side of that line.

To the engine the wall cells are 27 more bars and the two pars are washes, so
the `bars` group goes from 40 to 67 and `washes` from 28 to 30. A look that
drives every bar now lights the wall with it. Use `led_wall` to address the
wall alone. It can show colour, a chase or a wipe, not video.

In the 3D view each cell is drawn as a batten on a wall bracket, not as a
panel. **Not looked at:** the view could not be run in the session that
added the wall, so how the wall reads from the DJ, dance-floor and front
cameras is unchecked. The heights above come from reading the camera and
booth code, not from a render.

## Groups

The preset names groups a look can select on. `create_venue_rig` registers
them in the engine next to its own (`movers`, `washes`, `bars`, `strobes`,
`all`, `left`, `right` and so on). A preset group may not take one of those
names: the engine looks its groups up by name and the preset's would replace
it. So the washes are `centre_washes` and the strobes `truss_strobes`.

| Group | Fixtures | What |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `floor_spots` | 22 | The dance-floor spots |
| `spots_chord_a` to `spots_chord_d` | 5, 6, 6, 5 | One truss each, A upstage to D at the rear |
| `spots_left`, `spots_right` | 10, 10 | By side. The two heads on the centre line are in neither |
| `balcony_spots` | 4 | Mezzanine soffit |
| `centre_washes` | 8 | Moving washes at 10 ft |
| `truss_strobes` | 8 | |
| `led_pars` | 10 | All pars: `wall_pars` (6), `staircase_pars` (2), `booth_wash` (2) |
| `led_strips` | 8 | All strips: `booth_strips` (4), `skybox_strips` (4) |
| `dome_pixels` | 44 | The whole dome: `dome_spokes` (32) and `dome_rim` (12) |
| `dome_spokes_inner`, `dome_spokes_outer` | 16, 16 | The two rings of spoke cells |
| `led_wall` | 27 | `led_wall_centre` (15), `led_wall_wings` (12) |
| `dj_booth` | 33 | Everything at the booth: wall, booth strips, booth wash |

Every fixture is in at least one group, and the patch sheets list each
fixture's groups. These are this system's names, not the venue's console
groups. The demo rig payload still sends the fixture type as `group_name`, as
it does for every demo rig.

## Room shell

The preset carries a description of the room (`room` on the preset, sent as
`room_shell` by `/api/demo/rig`). Metres, origin at the centre of the floor.
Only the dome's diameter is from the press.

| Part | Data | Basis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dome | 88 ft across (radius 13.4 m), a spherical cap from 7.2 m at the rim to 9.1 m at the centre | Diameter sourced; heights assumed, set to clear the dome fixtures |
| Dance floor | Circle, radius 7.5 m, under the dome centre | Assumed |
| Balcony / mezzanine | Half ring round the rear of the room, 10.0 to 13.4 m out, soffit at 4.0 m, deck at 4.4 m | A mezzanine is reported; shape and size assumed |
| DJ booth | Box 6.5 x 1.3 m, 1.25 m high, against the upstage wall | A booth is reported; size and position assumed |
| Rear stage riser | Box 7.0 x 1.8 m, 0.6 m high, at the rear between the staircase pars | A rear level that converts to a stage is reported; size assumed |
| LED walls | Three panels on the upstage wall above the booth, 6 m and 2 x 2 m wide, from 3.2 to 5.6 m | Walls reported; size assumed |

The two outer mezzanine spots moved in half a metre (x = 7.5, was 8.0) so
that all four hang inside the balcony ring.

**TODO: the 3D view does not draw the shell yet.** The data is on the
endpoint but nothing in `frontend/` reads `room_shell`: the frontend could
not be compiled or tested in the session that added it, and a new WebGL
component was not shipped blind onto the default rig. The room is still the
box described above. To draw it:

- add `room_shell?` to the `Rig` type and pass it to `ClubRoom`;
- dome: a translucent spherical cap with its own material (`depthWrite`
  off), hidden when the camera is above it, as the ceiling is. The view's
  flat ceiling is at 9.2 m for this rig, just over the 9.1 m apex;
- balcony: a half ring slab between soffit and deck height;
- rear riser: a box. The view's dance floor and crowd run back to about
  z = 13.2, so they need to stop at the riser's front edge (z = 11.6);
- booth: the view already builds one in the same place and of the same
  size, so the shell's booth needs no drawing.

Nothing in the shell stops a beam. Beams land on the box's walls, floor and
flat ceiling, as before.

## Patch sheets

`scripts/export_demo_patch_sheet.py` writes two files from the preset, and a
test holds each committed copy to what the preset generates:

- `liv_miami_approx_patch.csv`: the rig CSV export's columns, then Stands
  For, Basis, Fixture ID, Groups and Rotation X / Y / Z.
- `liv_miami_approx_patch.md`: universes, groups, then one row per fixture
  with ID, name, profile, mode, channels, universe, address, group, groups,
  position and rotation.

Rotation is 0 for every fixture. The preset does not turn fixtures: the 3D
view mounts and aims each one from where it is patched.

## Not modelled

- Video on the LED walls. The cells above are a lighting stand-in.
- The backlit skyboxes, the glowing staircase and the three bars, beyond the
  strips and pars placed near them.
- The rear VIP level's stage conversion, beyond the riser in the room shell.
- Haze, lasers, CO2 or any effect the sources do not list.
