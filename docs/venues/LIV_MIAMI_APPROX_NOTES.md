# LIV Miami (approximate recreation) - notes

**APPROXIMATE RECREATION of LIV Miami (Fontainebleau Miami Beach), built from
public 2009 trade press. It is NOT LIV's real patch, plot or addressing, and is
not endorsed by or affiliated with the venue.**

Nothing here came from the venue, its designers or its console. Positions,
addresses, universe split and several fixture counts are invented so the room
can be used as a test rig. Do not use this sheet to patch, address or programme
anything in the real room.

- Preset: `liv_miami_approx` in `backend/app/services/demo/venue_presets.py`
- Patch sheet: `docs/venues/liv_miami_approx_patch.csv`, generated by
  `scripts/export_demo_patch_sheet.py` (edit the preset, then re-run it)
- It is the default demo rig: `DEMO_VENUE_PRESET` in `backend/app/main.py` and
  `DEFAULT_DEMO_PRESET` in `backend/app/api/endpoints/demo.py`

project write-up, as summarised in the brief this was built from. The articles
were not re-read while building this, so treat each line as "reported", not
checked.

## What is assumed

The press gives fixture types for these but no counts or positions:

- **Opti Tri Pars (8):** three up each side wall and two at the foot of the
  staircase, all on the floor.
- **DLED 60 strips (8):** four across the DJ booth front, two on each side
  wall at skybox height.
- **Atomic 3000 strobes (8):** one off each end of each of the four spot
  trusses.
- **Spoke count (16) and rim segment count (12):** the press gives node and
  flood totals, not how they are divided.
- **Every height and every X/Z position.** Only the 10 ft wash trim and the
  88 ft dome are from the sources. The 6.5 m spot trim, the dome rising from
  7.0 m at the rim to 8.6 m near the centre, and the 4.0 m mezzanine soffit are
  guesses at a plausible room.
- **All DMX addressing and the universe split.**

## What was changed to fit this system

- **The dome is folded down.** 885 dome fixtures patched one for one would
  need about 21 universes at this system's 12-channel footprint; rig universes
  stop at 15, and the 3D view would be drawing 885 lights. So the dome is 44
  fixtures. Each one stands for the group named in the patch sheet's "Stands
  For" column. The matrix can show a radial chase or a two-ring pulse, not
  video.
- **The spots hang on four straight trusses, not a ring.** The engine groups
  moving heads by depth and spreads each movement across a row; the 3D view
  draws one truss per row. A ring of 22 would be eleven rows of two heads with
  nothing to fan across. The four chords (5, 6, 6 and 5 heads) fit inside the
  dome circle. The washes are two rows of four for the same reason.
- **The dome cells and rim segments are patched as generic bars and pars.**
  That is the closest type the preset vocabulary has. In the 3D view they are
  drawn as battens and par cans hung from the ceiling on pipes, which is not
  what a cove node or a flood looks like.
- **The room is a box in the 3D view.** The view builds a rectangular room
  from the stage size; it has no round walls or dome. The fixtures sit on the
  dome's circle but the walls around them are square.
- **The booth is upstage (-Z) and the mezzanine is at the rear (+Z).** That is
  this system's convention, not a claim about which way the real room faces.

## Modernised

The sources describe the 2009 installation. The room has been refurbished
since and the current rig was not researched, so this is not a claim about what
hangs there today. Where a 2009 fixture does not fit how this system drives a
rig, it is patched as the modern LED fixture of the same job:

| 2009 fixture | Patched as | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Design Spot 575E / 300E (discharge lamp, mechanical colour) | Generic LED moving spot with RGBW mixing, 16 / 12 channels | The demo profiles mix colour in RGBW; a lamp fixture's wheel and mechanical dimmer are not modelled |
| Impression RGB | Generic RGBW moving wash with zoom, 14 channels | Adds the white emitter current washes have |
| Atomic 3000 DMX (xenon) | Generic 4-channel LED strobe | No xenon recharge or duty-cycle limit is modelled |
| iColor Cove MX, ColorBlast 12 (RGB) | Generic 12-channel RGBW+A+UV cells on DMX | The originals are fed through Color Kinetics power/data supplies, which this system does not model |
| Opti Tri Par, DLED 60 strip (RGB) | Generic 12-channel RGBW+A+UV par / bar | Same footprint as every other static fixture in the demo rigs |

Every fixture therefore uses this system's generic demo channel map for its
type. None uses the source fixture's real DMX personality, so the addresses in
the patch sheet would not drive the real fixtures even if the positions were
right.

## Not modelled

- The LED walls at the DJ booth (video, not lighting fixtures).
- The backlit skyboxes, the glowing staircase and the three bars, beyond the
  strips and pars placed near them.
- The rear VIP level's stage conversion.
- Haze, lasers, CO2 or any effect the sources do not list.

## Universes

| Universe | Contents | Channels |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | 22 dance-floor spots, 4 mezzanine spots, 8 washes | 512 |
| 1 | 32 dome spoke cells, 8 strips, 8 strobes | 512 |
| 2 | 12 dome rim segments, 8 pars | 240 |

102 fixtures. Universes 0 and 1 are full: adding a fixture to either means
moving a group to universe 2 or a new universe in the preset.

As with every demo rig, DMX output is held off the network unless
`DEMO_RIG_DMX_OUTPUT` is set, and the demo endpoints refuse to apply it over a
patched rig.
